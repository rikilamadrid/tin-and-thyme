## Changes

- **Done cue:**
  - Done boards get a green dot or check and muted text in the Boards tabs.
  - Served slips on ORDERS get a completed treatment.
- **Working cue:** a subtle, state-driven loop (bob, steam or sparks, console blink) only for characters the model already marks as working. The loop is phase-offset per character so they don't move in sync.
- **MAIN:** a sign or marquee with gentle running lights, and a short celebratory flourish (for example a confetti puff or a light chase) tied to the existing confirmed-delivery animation window.
- **Crew panel collapse:**
  - The collapsed state lives in `src/store/ui.ts`, remembered in a try/catch-guarded `localStorage`.
  - A toggle button with an `aria-label` and `aria-expanded` sits on the panel edge.
  - A collapsed rail width replaces `CREW_W` wherever the canvas, scrim and dock compute their left inset.
- **Ambient objects:**
  - A small scheduler in the scene state: one object at a time, a random gap of about 2–6 minutes, and a pass of 6–12 seconds.
  - Objects are drawn behind the hull or inside the window panes only.
  - The scheduler is deterministic under a seed, so it can be tested.
- **Head chef:**
  - Idle sway and an occasional glance, plus a chair or stool at the console.
  - Badges keep their positions and priority.
- **Transport pod:**
  - Delete `kitchen()`. Move the transporter's drawn position and its in-tube and queue spots into the freed top-band area.
  - Restyle it as a pod with a pad, ring and glow.
  - Keep `BEAM_MS`, `BEAM_BUSY_MS`, queueing and every beam trigger unchanged.
  - Make sure walk paths reach the new spot.
- **Reduced motion:** every new loop checks `prefers-reduced-motion` and draws a static frame; ambient objects are off.
- **Performance:** no new per-frame allocations in hot loops, and nothing that keeps the canvas animating when the scene would otherwise be static.
