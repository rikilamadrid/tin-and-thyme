## Status

Complete

## Parent Feature

## Blocked by

## Goal

The kitchen fills the screen's height and its stations have room between them,
with less starfield showing above and below.

## Context

- Read: `src/galley/scene.ts` (`VIEW`, layout constants, `SPOTS`, `HANGOUTS`,
  `route`), `src/galley/GalleyCanvas.tsx` (fit/`bounds`, ~L70–L195).
- Relevant area: `src/galley/scene.ts`, `src/galley/GalleyCanvas.tsx`

## Changes

- Make the logical view taller and spread the stations, pods, the pass, the
  belt and the hatch vertically and horizontally to match.
- Keep routes, hit-testing and hangouts consistent with the new positions.

- Clicking each crew member and station still selects the right thing.
- `pnpm typecheck`.

## Notes / Decisions

- None
- Reconciled 2026-11-12 with Feature 09: this ticket owns the kitchen's height and spacing only. Ticket 09.5 later adds lifecycle stations, review spots and lounge places on top of this layout and must not redo it. Relevant area narrowed to the files this ticket names.
- Fit (repair 1, 2026-11-12): the taller view made pixel style clip in 1x windows about 596–684 px tall,
  because pixel art never went below 1 device pixel per unit. Below that, pixel style now draws at 1 on a
  larger canvas, and the browser scales the canvas down smoothly. It does not draw pixel art at a
  fractional scale under 1, where rows drop out and sprites round up past their neighbours. Both styles
  now shrink to 0.4 before clipping, so every size that fit at 7b3eda3 still fits.
- Horizontal spread (repair 1, 2026-11-12): the room keeps its 512-unit width, and that width is
  constrained. Two columns already fill it, split by the walking lane:
  - left: the prep line and the subagent stations, x 40–320 (five 48-wide pods);
  - centre: the lane, x 336;
  - right: the transporter, café and lounge, x 352–528.

  The pods cannot spread further without crowding the lane, and the café cannot narrow without losing its
  stools. Widening the room would shrink the kitchen whenever a docked drawer makes the fit width-bound,
  and that is the case where this ticket's height gain shows. So the horizontal spread is limited to two
  changes:
  - the pod pitch, 56 → 58;
  - the pass: reviewers now stand ±46 from the scanner instead of −37/+37 (`PASS_DX`), so their name tags
    clear the scanner hood. They overlapped it at 7b3eda3.
