### Implementation notes (2026-11-13)

- The "local commits only; no push, PR" note above is superseded by the human's 2026-11-13 delivery authorization: ticket-branch push and a non-draft PR to `main`. Merge stays with the human.
- Files: `src/data/fixture/crewFixture.ts` (fixture, invariant checks, scenarios) and `scripts/crew-scenarios.ts` (runner). The contract (`crewContract.ts`, `crew.ts`, `docs/crew-contract.md`) is unchanged. Run with `pnpm exec tsx scripts/crew-scenarios.ts` (`--list` lists without running).
- Every result is tagged with its evidence kind. The runner prints FIXTURE, LIVE and MOCK sections separately; this ticket produces fixture results only, and the live section lists the three live acceptance items as not run (09.8).
- Fixture data is labelled in the data itself: books are keyed `fixture:crew-lifecycle` and session ids start with `fx`. The fixture is not wired into `?fixture=` or the toolbar: `busyFixture.ts` and the UI are outside this ticket.
- **Decision for 09.3:** `crewFixtureSnapshot` is the fixture's own reading of the contract's snapshot section, because 09.3 has not published the real projection. Two readings are the fixture's: `stateSince` uses the after-restart rule, and `lastOutcome` is the outcome of the character's latest ended session. 09.3 should confirm or replace both.
- **Decision for 09.6:** the contract has no function that writes `CrewSession.telemetry`, so the fixture sets that field by hand. Everything else changes the book only through the contract's functions.
- Restart scenarios always open the restore pass first (`migrateLegacy` or `beginRestore`) and run each in forward and reverse restore order, as the 09.1 review asked.
- `crewBookProblems`, `crewHistoryProblems` and `crewSnapshotProblems` take any book or snapshot, so 09.8 can run them on live data.
- **Not covered, and reported as such by the runner:** the acceptance item "both art styles, reduced motion, and sound on and off" is checked at data level only. How states are drawn, the reduced-motion poses and the audio behaviour are visual or audible checks for 09.2, 09.5, 09.6 and 09.8. The disk side of a restart (backup, atomic write) belongs to 09.3.
